ToggleTax
Shopify Theme App Extension

Adds a toggle widget to your store that lets customers switch between tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive prices — on product pages and in the cart. No backend, no servers.

How to set up
  1. Go to Online Store → Themes → Edit theme
  2. Navigate to a Product page in the editor
  3. Click Add block → Apps → ToggleTax
  4. Set your Tax Rate (%) (e.g. 20 for 20% VAT / GST)
  5. Enable "Theme base prices include tax" if your store prices already include tax
  6. Save — done! Cart updates automatically
